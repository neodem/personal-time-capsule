# Who am I

My name is Vincent. I am a human. This is the one document that is not touched by AI. 

For almost 40 years now I've been saving my life digitally, thinking that it would go to my kids when I died. As I'm getting older I'm realizing that my kids won't really have a need for my million emails or some writing I did in college. But then I realized that historians in 50-100 years would care. Even the most pedestrian emails or photos or whatever could be useful to some historian trying to understand this time and not satisfied by the "official record" or what was published by the news.

If hundreds or thousands of us could archive all of our stuff in some way and vault it away for 50 years or so, we could be doing historians a favor. 

But how to do it? To create a full specification and code it up is an overwhelming task for a human who works 9-5 each day. Thankfully there is now AI. I've been thinking for a while how to use this new supertool to do something useful and I decided this would be it. So this repo is definitely been helped along by AI writing, researching and yes some designing. But I have been over every word of what is published (so far) and I promise to continue being a steward of this collaborative effort between me and the AI.
