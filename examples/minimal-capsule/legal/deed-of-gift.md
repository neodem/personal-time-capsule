# DEED OF GIFT

This Deed of Gift ("Deed") is made on 2026-08-30, between:

**Donor:** Jane Smith, of [address omitted in this illustrative example] ("Donor")

and

**Recipient:** Springfield Historical Society, of [address omitted in this illustrative example] ("Custodian").

## 1. The Gift

Donor hereby gives, transfers, and delivers to Custodian, as an unconditional gift, custody of the digital archive described below (the "Capsule"), subject to the terms of this Deed.

- **Capsule identifier:** urn:uuid:7c2e9f10-4b3d-4e2a-9a71-5d8f3c1b2e40
- **Date sealed:** 2026-08-30
- **Brief description:** A minimal, illustrative Personal Time Capsule demonstrating the PTC-0.1 directory structure and `capsule.yaml` format.
- **Manifest reference:** see `manifest-sha256.txt` and `tagmanifest-sha256.txt` in this bag, as finalized at sealing.

This Deed applies only to the Capsule as it existed at the time of sealing referenced above. It does not extend to any other capsule Donor may create, or to any later, separately sealed version.

## 2. What Is Transferred

Custodian receives:

(a) Physical and digital custody of the Capsule;

(b) The right and obligation to preserve, verify, and replicate the Capsule in accordance with the technical practices the Capsule itself records (see `INTEGRITY.md` and `CUSTODY.md` in the Personal Time Capsule specification) and any separate Custodian Agreement executed between the parties;

(c) A non-exclusive license to reproduce, migrate the format of, and provide access to the Capsule's contents, strictly in accordance with the release policy recorded in this Capsule's own `capsule.yaml` (see its `access` section and the `email` collection's override) and any Access Restriction Agreement executed alongside this Deed.

This Deed does not transfer copyright — see Section 3.

## 3. Copyright and Ownership

Donor retains all copyright and other intellectual property rights in the content of the Capsule. This Deed transfers custody of the Capsule and the license described in Section 2 only. Upon Donor's death, copyright passes to Donor's heirs, successors, or assigns under applicable law, subject to the license granted in Section 2, which survives Donor's death and continues to bind Donor's heirs, successors, and assigns.

## 4. Third-Party Material

Donor acknowledges that the Capsule may contain material referencing, depicting, or authored in part by persons other than Donor ("Third Parties") who have not separately consented to the Capsule's creation — for example, the correspondent named in `data/email/2019-07-14-family-reunion.eml`. Donor represents that, to the best of Donor's knowledge, Donor has the lawful right to include such material as part of a personal archive of this kind. **Donor makes no representation that every Third Party referenced in the Capsule has consented to its creation or inclusion.** The parties acknowledge that privacy protection for Third Parties is instead addressed through the privacy zones, redaction, and access restrictions recorded in the Capsule itself (see `privacy-overrides.yaml`) and in any Access Restriction Agreement — not through a warranty in this Deed.
