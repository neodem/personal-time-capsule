# Biography — Jane Smith

Jane Smith was born in 1978 in Columbus, Ohio, and has lived in the American Midwest for most of her life. She trained as a public librarian and worked at the Franklin County Public Library from 2001 until her retirement in 2043. She raised two children, Alex and Priya, and was an enthusiastic amateur photographer for over four decades.
