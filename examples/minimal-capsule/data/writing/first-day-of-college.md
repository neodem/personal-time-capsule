# First day of college

*Written September 1996*

I don't think I slept much last night. Dad drove me up this morning with the car packed so full I could barely see out the back window. Move-in was chaos — three families all trying to use the same elevator — but my roommate seems nice enough, and she already has her side of the room covered in band posters I've never heard of.

I keep telling myself I'll write in here more than I actually will. We'll see.
